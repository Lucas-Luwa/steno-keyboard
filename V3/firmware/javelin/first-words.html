Your first words on Steno V3

Javelin + Lapwing · USB at the top · release every key to finish a stroke
Esc#TPH* TabSKWR*
*FPLTD *RBGSZ
# A OE U #
  1. Click the typing box below with your mouse.
  2. Tap S: left-hand lower row, second key from the left. Expect is.
  3. Tap T: left-hand upper row, third key from the left. Expect it.
  4. Press the purple H and L together, then release both. H = left upper row, fifth key. L = right upper row, fourth key. Expect hello.
  5. Tap any * to undo the last stroke. Expect hello to disappear.

Everything typed here stays in this local page.

Esc and Tab are your two extra far-left switches. LEDs start off in this first Javelin setup. The light demos are saved separately in the repo.
